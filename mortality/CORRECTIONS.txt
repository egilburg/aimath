Corrections — mortality, report revision 2026-09-10-r4

Report an error or missing attribution through the Correction report form at
https://github.com/egilburg/aimath/issues . Include the immutable edition/commit,
theorem/page or Lean declaration, the proposed correction and checkable evidence.
No monitoring or response-time promise is made.

Reports are assessed against the written statement, formal encoding and cited
literature. A successful Lean build alone cannot dismiss a scope or attribution
error. Confirmed corrections receive a public notice and forward revision,
crediting substantive contributions as appropriate. Earlier editions and tags
are retained. A central claim found unreliable is identified as such while
investigated; unaffected claims are distinguished. No professional review is
implied by public release.

R3 introduced the mathematical improvement; r4 changes presentation and disclosure only. See CHANGES.txt.
