PAPER-FOCUSED MORTALITY FORMALIZATION

The mathematical source is now the seven-module FiniteMonoidMortality library.
It proves the same three published endpoints, with unchanged assumptions,
conclusions and numerical bound. Publication.lean imports its Main module.

Reading order and purpose

1. MatrixWords: chronological matrix-word multiplication and scalar extension.
   This is the only word convention used in the paper; no digit/radix notions
   or unrelated word-density predicates are imported.
2. ReachableSpans: the linear-word orbit and ascending bounded orbit spans.
   Only the ambient-dimension spanning result needed for a short observation
   is retained; observable rows, forbidden contexts and broader orbit-mortality
   developments are omitted.
3. Compression: rank-sized factorization H = U V, rank/unit lemmas and the
   compressed return V Y U. Minimum-rank immortal-semigroup applications and
   multiple-reset boundary observations are omitted.
4. InvariantForms: the finite unit group, the sum of g g^T, its positive trace,
   and the trace-defect certificate for rank loss in a sandwich.
5. QuadraticObservation: symmetric coordinates, their n(n+1)/2 dimension,
   the affine linear lift and a short nonzero observation.
6. Descent: the exact recurrence L -> 2L + D, a singular initial generator,
   and induction on rank. Both outside copies of the word remain charged.
7. Main: the short rank-decreasing sandwich, real mortality and its rational
   specialization. No finiteness assumption is added on the generator type.

Scope of the refactor

The previous publication contained 102 mathematical modules, 15,695 lines
and 675,198 bytes. The focused library has 7 modules, 1,038 lines and
43,554 bytes after explicit Mathlib import fixes. The final
machine-readable counts are recorded separately. Definitions, theorem
statements and proof terms written in retained declarations were extracted
from the prior development and renamed consistently, not replaced by
unproved wrappers around the original library. No original project imports
remain. The 66 retained declaration bodies compare exactly with the originals
after the explicit identifier mapping and removal of comments/whitespace.

Verification and compatibility

Audit.lean prints all three endpoint statements, their transitive axioms and
the mortality bound. Specification.lean reproduces the original endpoint
statements and the complete custom definition closure appearing in those
statements (matrixWord and finiteMortalityBound); each original claim is
proved directly by its refactored endpoint. These are compile-checked
compatibility theorems, not assumed equalities. The public dependency pins
remain unchanged, so Matrix.rank, real/rational fields, lists and arithmetic
continue to refer to the same Mathlib definitions.

The declaration map and source-equivalence report provide an additional,
reproducible record of the extraction. The manuscript continues to supply
mathematical attribution and the distinction between named formal endpoints
and written corollaries. This refactor adds no mathematical novelty claim.

The retained final build starts with an empty project .lake/build and sets
LAKE_ARTIFACT_CACHE=false to prevent restoration of prior project artifacts.
All seven modules and the Publication entry point visibly compile in the
recorded build.log. The only build warning is the original main proof's
style suggestion to replace letI with let; it does not affect correctness.
