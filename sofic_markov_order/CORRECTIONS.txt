CORRECTIONS

https://github.com/egilburg/aimath/issues

Choose Correction report and identify this paper, version 2026-09-08-r1, its
permanent commit URL, the exact theorem/page/declaration and checkable evidence.
Reports remain allegations until assessed. No response-time promise is made.

Confirmed corrections will identify the affected version and evidence, credit
substantive help when appropriate, and be recorded with a dated forward revision.

immutable version. A failed central claim should be marked unreliable or withdrawn
on the current landing page and affected version's correction notice. Recheck
affected proof, prose and citations before issuing a correction. Do not treat
unrelated results as invalid without evidence.

are recorded at this initial release; this is not an assertion of error-freedom.
