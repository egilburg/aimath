The formal exact-order formulation proves D-step Markovity and failure for all k<D; this gives the prose minimum. The formal construction uses D−1+n² letters, with one single-entry residual letter for each matrix coordinate. The n=2 critical word is empty and its matrix is I₂. Public dependency software is not mathematical peer review. No global priority, fixed-alphabet optimality or first-formalization claim is in the supported result.
